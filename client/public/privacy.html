Privacy Policy — Habits

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Habits ("the app") is a habit-tracking application. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, and either a password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text) or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID and the email address Google shares with us.
  • Habit data you create: habit names, colors, schedule, completion history, and any amounts you log for measurable habits.
  • Reminder settings: the reminder times you set, used only to schedule local notifications on your own device.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or any device data beyond what's needed to keep you signed in and sync your habits.

How we use your information

Solely to run the app: authenticate you, store and sync your habits across devices, and send the reminder notifications you configure. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or other third parties.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we use Google's Sign-In service only to verify your identity and obtain your email address. We don't access your Google contacts, files, or any other Google data.

Data storage

Your data is stored on our server, secured behind an authenticated API. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device and never leave it.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account, inside the app. This immediately and irreversibly removes your habits, logs, and account information from our server.

Children's privacy

Habits is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at support@amsoft.am.
